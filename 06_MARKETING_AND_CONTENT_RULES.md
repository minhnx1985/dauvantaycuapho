# 06 — MARKETING & CONTENT RULES

## Conversion intent

The landing page should persuade through:
- clarity;
- beauty;
- specificity;
- intellectual and cultural relevance.

Not through:
- aggressive sales pressure;
- fake scarcity;
- hypey copy.

## Priority hierarchy

1. Show the book.
2. Explain the idea.
3. Establish the author.
4. Show event relevance.
5. Reduce risk via sample.
6. Make purchase easy.

## Tone

- literary
- urban-cultural
- calm
- informed
- persuasive but restrained

## What to emphasize

- the unique interpretive hook: “vân tay” of urban cultural spaces;
- Hà Nội as layered and plural;
- the author's long engagement with Hà Nội;
- sketches and fieldwork;
- launch event as an occasion for conversation.

## What to avoid

- turning the book into a dry academic monograph;
- turning the page into a souvenir-shop landing page;
- empty praise with no specifics;
- overusing “Hà Nội thân thương”, “hồn phố”, etc. in generic ways;
- overloading the page with too many book covers or too much biography.

## CTA strategy

Primary CTA should appear in:
- Hero
- after author/event/sample cluster
- final section

The CTA label may be:
- “Mua sách”
- “Xem tại Nhã Nam” if product page is informational.

## Event UX

The launch section must be legible and actionable.
Do not hide it behind a modal or carousel.
Treat it as a core selling point, not a footer note.
