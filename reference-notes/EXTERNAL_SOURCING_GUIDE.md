# EXTERNAL SOURCING GUIDE

## A. Portraits to source

### Nguyễn Hoàng Diệu Thủy
Suggested first target:
- Nhã Nam author page: `https://nhanam.vn/nguyen-hoang-dieu-thuy`

Possible backup sources:
- reputable news coverage of her books/interviews.

### TS. Trần Ngọc Hiếu
Suggested search framing:
- `"Trần Ngọc Hiếu" giảng viên khoa Ngữ văn Đại học Sư phạm Hà Nội`
- `"Tiến sĩ Trần Ngọc Hiếu"`

Prefer:
- university pages;
- publisher pages;
- reputable interviews with clear portrait rights context.

## B. Other-book covers by Nguyễn Trương Quý
Try these sources in order:
1. Nhã Nam pages if title exists there
2. reputable bookstore pages (Tiki/Vinabook/NetaBooks/Phương Nam/Ben Nghe when applicable)
3. reputable news articles that show the book cover clearly
