## B. Core positioning

This book is not a nostalgic coffee-table object only.
It is a literary-cultural exploration of Hà Nội through its distinct urban spaces.

The central proposition from source text:
- each area of Hà Nội bears a different cultural fingerprint;
- urban districts, streets, waterways, habitations and cultural institutions together shape the city;
- the book reads Hà Nội through geography, architecture, everyday life and cultural memory;
- the sketches used in the book are part of the author's fieldwork.

## C. Approved content themes from supplied author text

You may state that the book:
- identifies the cultural nature of Hà Nội through real urban spaces;
- reflects on how administrative boundaries changed while deeper cultural zones remained legible;
- observes the city through small but meaningful details;
- moves from the ancient quarter and villa zones to Trúc Bạch, Mơ - Sét, Tô Lịch and other cultural routes;
- treats each area like a different fingerprint of the city;
- is the result of many years of observation, thinking, writing and sketching.

## D. Do not overclaim

Do not claim the book is:
- a definitive academic history of Hà Nội;
- a formal atlas;
- a full encyclopedia;
- a purely nostalgic memoir.

Safer framing:
- tản văn / tiểu luận / tiểu địa chí văn hóa;
- a field-based literary-cultural reading of Hà Nội;
- essays and reflections grounded in urban spaces.
