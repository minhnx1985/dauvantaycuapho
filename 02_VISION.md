# 02 — VISUAL & EXPERIENCE VISION

## Core concept

**A quiet literary landing page that feels like a walk through Hà Nội.**

The cover already gives the visual system:
- paper-like ivory background;
- hand-painted urban scene;
- muted greens, dusty blue-greys, warm beige light;
- delicate, slightly calligraphic title typography;
- editorial calm, not commercial noise.

The website should extend this world.

---

## Visual DNA

### Surface
- warm off-white / paper ivory;
- subtle paper grain optional, but restrained.

### Ink
- dark brown-charcoal and soft black;
- slightly warm neutral text color.

### Accent palette from cover art
- tree green;
- desaturated teal / blue-grey;
- warm stone beige;
- faded terracotta/rust (from the signed-edition seal and event poster typography);
- dusty olive.

### Image language
Combine three image modes:
1. **cover-led hero**
2. **painted/sketched city fragments from the sample PDF**
3. **author / speaker portraits**

The result should feel editorial and cultural.

Avoid:
- heavy gradients;
- glassmorphism;
- neon UI;
- “travel booking” aesthetics;
- “city guide app” UI;
- map-pin icon overload.

---

## Typography

Use at most two families:
- a refined serif with excellent Vietnamese support for headings;
- a readable serif or sans companion for body/meta.

Tone:
- literary;
- intellectually warm;
- calm;
- elegant.

Do not imitate the cover title with a tacky decorative font.

---

## Layout feeling

Editorial, spacious, asymmetrical when useful.
The landing page should feel closer to a feature essay than a marketplace grid.

Allow:
- large cover display;
- chapter/section framing;
- pull quotes;
- painterly crops;
- quiet dividers;
- circular speaker portraits as requested.

Avoid:
- too many cards;
- crowded carousels;
- product-detail clutter;
- giant badges or fake urgency.

---

## Motion

Minimal and tasteful:
- soft reveal;
- slight parallax on artwork if performance survives;
- gentle hover on CTA/book mockup.

Honor `prefers-reduced-motion`.
