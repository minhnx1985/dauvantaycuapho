## C. Marketing psychology rules

Use:
1. immediate object salience — show the book early;
2. specificity — chapters, spaces, examples;
3. authority — author with real profile;
4. aesthetic trust — coherent visual world;
5. risk reduction — sample PDF;
6. event relevance — social proof/context;
7. repeated CTA at high-intent moments.

Avoid:
- fake scarcity;
- countdowns;
- exaggerated “must-read” hype;
- generic sentimental copy;
- salesy popups.
