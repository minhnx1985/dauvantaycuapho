## Deliberate sourcing limitation
The suggested Nhã Nam page for Nguyễn Hoàng Diệu Thủy (`https://nhanam.vn/tac-gia/nguyen-hoang-dieu-thuy`) returned HTTP 404 during source validation. Search results did not provide a trustworthy, directly attributable portrait for Nguyễn Hoàng Diệu Thủy or TS. Trần Ngọc Hiếu. The event section therefore uses typographic circular identity markers for those two speakers, not fabricated or unverified photographs. This keeps names and roles accurate while avoiding questionable external assets.

## Other books
The “Các sách khác” shelf uses typographic editorial covers rather than misattributed external cover images. This is intentional because exact cover/source matches could not be verified within the available source pass.
